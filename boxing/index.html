Slop Boxing · HP 100
VR: put the controllers down to box with your bare hands (hand tracking), or use controllers. Make a fist to hit hard; an open hand only slaps. His glove glows before he throws: block it with your fists, or slip/duck where your head was. Hit him while his guard is down. Bump your fists together to restart after a knockdown.
Desktop: left/right click = left/right punch at the cursor (or J/K) · Q/E = slip · C = duck · R = restart after a knockdown
